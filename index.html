Phrase Gym

Practise saying useful English phrases from your lessons, videos, and conversations. Each card starts with a situation and a hidden answer. Say the phrase from memory, hear the reference, and rate your recall to schedule the next review.

Start with installation, then add your phrases. The Practice, Library, Coach, and Settings tabs switch pages. The Phrase Gym name returns to Practice. Reloading keeps the selected page, and every page shows the installed version.

Daily and extra practice

On the Practice page, choose All lessons or select a lesson. You can type to narrow the lesson list. The due and new counts show what is available within today's remaining limits. Start begins or resumes Daily. It mixes the longest-overdue phrases and a random selection of new phrases, up to the two daily limits. The defaults are 20 due and five new. Changing a limit in Settings takes effect when you return to practice.

  • Try saying the phrase, even when the card is new. Use New cue, Hint, or More explanation where available. Reveal shows the answer and reference audio.
  • Rate what you said before looking at the answer or asking for help. If you could not recall the phrase, choose Again. The first rating starts its schedule. Again, Hard, Good, and Easy show when the phrase would come back, such as 3d or 2.5m. A phrase already graded today has no new interval to preview.
  • Use Skip to continue without an assessment or practice credit. Previous card lets you revisit earlier revealed cards from this run. A skipped card can still be graded, and a graded card shows its saved rating and accepts another take. The right chevron moves back toward the current card. More actions lets you delete the displayed phrase after confirmation.

After a pass, Extra starts a fresh one when there is more eligible material beyond today's allowance. A press extends each reached limit by one increment. For example, a limit of 20 due reviews becomes 40 once you've completed 20. Pressing again without further practice gives no additional allowance. A fresh pass can offer skipped cards again. Phrases scheduled for a future date stay out of Daily and Extra. When no phrases are available, Start is disabled and Practice can show the next due date.

Record and compare

Tap the microphone to record, then tap it again to finish. Finishing a take on the front reveals the answer and plays the reference followed by your recording. Rehearsing on the back uses the same order. After you press Ref on that card, subsequent takes on its answer side play only your recording automatically. Use Mine for your latest take or Compare to hear both.

Every completed take stays in the browser while it uploads in the background. You can rate or move on while it uploads. Switching tabs finishes and keeps the take, and Start resumes the regular card. A failed upload stays on the device for retry. The saved take includes the cue, help, and answer shown during the attempt.

Choose Recording history in the revealed card's More actions menu or use the history icon in Library phrase details. Takes appear newest first, with the date and a Mine button. Close the practice history with Close or Escape.

Find and practise one phrase

The Library lists phrases by lesson. Search across lessons by wording, meaning, situation, or use. Open a phrase to see its meaning, lesson, source, reference audio, next due date, and saved takes. Looking up a phrase leaves its schedule alone. If you are looking for new material, use Send “…” to Coach or Not it? from the search results.

Choose Practice in phrase details to open the same hidden-answer card, then rate or skip to return to the details. This practice is available outside the daily allowance. The first rating of the phrase on a learning day can reschedule it even before its due date.

Delete removes the phrase, reference audio, and saved recordings after confirmation. Practice history keeps the wording of completed phrases. A take still waiting to upload stays on the device until you discard it. If deleting files fails, use Retry cleanup.

Track practice and adjust settings

Below the Practice controls, Today shows completed recalls, new phrases, ratings, and recordings, with pending takes counted separately. See today’s phrases includes wording from cards you completed and later deleted. Progress adds up your practice days, ratings, and recordings. Total shows how many phrases are due now, due later, or new. It also shows a long-term memory count based on the scheduler's estimate of each phrase's stability.

A day with at least one rating counts toward the streak. The current streak still shows yesterday's run until you practise today. Missing a full day resets the current streak. The best streak and practice history stay visible. Set a weekly target of one to six days in Settings and pause or change it any time. Each Monday starts a fresh week. The target affects only the progress display.

Settings also holds your native language, learning timezone, daily limits, and return pace. Pick Often, Balanced, or Rarely. The choices show how often a phrase rated Good each time would come back in its first month and year.

Use another device

On an iPhone, open your private HTTPS address in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. The icon opens Phrase Gym without the browser toolbar and still needs the running server. Android browsers that support app shortcuts also offer Coach from the icon. The pages follow your device's light or dark appearance.

The server runs on macOS and Linux. Use a macOS browser for the currently supported audio playback and recording workflow. The server listens only on loopback by default and keeps learner data in its local application-data directory.

See backup and restore for maintenance and the HTTP API reference for external agents.

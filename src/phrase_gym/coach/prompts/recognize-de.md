The learning language is German. Capitalisation is part of the word: a capitalised noun and a
lowercase word with the same letters, such as Essen (food) and essen (to eat), are different
valid words, so keep the capitalisation as typed. A noun written in lowercase directly after an
article, such as "die tür", is an obvious typo: correct only its first letter. Keep a bare noun
as typed and do not add an article. A separable verb split in its sentence, such as einkaufen in
"Ich kaufe heute ein", has no contiguous source_span: leave source_span empty.

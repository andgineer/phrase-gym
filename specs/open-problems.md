# Unverified acceptance

These are concrete uncertainties, not prerequisites for an infrastructure project. The [delivery ladder](plan/implementation-plan.md) assigns them to the relevant increment.

- **Learning transfer:** [instruction studies](research/automatic-use.md) show increased expression use in unscripted classroom speech, but lasting everyday transfer for this adult personal trainer remains unverified. Daily and Extra remain phrase recall; a distinct freer-speaking activity still needs functional design before implementation.
- **Sustained engagement:** progress feedback, the streak and the forgiving weekly target are research-informed choices, not demonstrated long-term adherence for Phrase Gym. Whether the learner enjoys the weekly target and keeps practising with it is unmeasured and is not inferred from practice counts. Keep them independent of memory grades and make return after a break easy.
- **First MVP:** the software loop and stopped-app copy procedure are implemented. A later real-clock return, semantic acceptance of representative external cards and complete attended MacBook recording/replay remain unverified. Routine external imports do not require listening to source recordings.
- **Practice facts:** Today counts, the studied list, the streak, the weekly target and the new/due counts are verified against a simulated clock. A real-clock next-day return, missed day and return after a missed week, and the panel and weekly target Settings on a real iPhone, remain attended checks.
- **Prepared content:** externally prepared chunk cards need fresh semantic inspection for useful target boundaries, natural completed gap cues, competing answers, varied partial-wording hints that help recall the exact chunk, complete source coverage, and preservation of the source's phrase-to-recording association. The revised preparation instructions have not yet been validated in a fresh import session. No fixed synthetic pack, including the documented example, establishes every course's content quality.
- **Speech:** the existing internal Coach Piper voice remains a candidate for learner preference. External agents choose an available authorized TTS tool and natural voice when audio is missing; supplied audio is reused without a speech-analysis acceptance gate. Attended listening is a separate learner-requested activity. Serbian help preferences remain unresolved and do not block English.
- **Recording:** the learner has verified Safari recording/reference playback on the Mac and the iPhone recording/playback fix on the exercised path. Complete saved replay after reload and application restart, final-word completeness across varied speech lengths, headset routing, iPhone background interruptions and Daily, Extra and phrase-specific Practice, Library search, details and Delete on a real iPhone still need attended checks. Synthetic browser audio cannot certify those paths.
- **Personal data:** stopped-app copy/restore is covered with local data; the learner should retain a private backup before relying on it. This does not require concurrent recovery.
- **Coach cards:** attended listening to Coach's Piper references, a real recording on the MacBook and a real-clock next-day recall of Coach cards remain unverified. Words typed from echo-words have not been tried. No provider/platform qualification matrix is implied.

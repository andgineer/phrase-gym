# Unverified acceptance

These are concrete uncertainties, not prerequisites for an infrastructure project. The [delivery ladder](plan/implementation-plan.md) assigns them to the relevant increment.

- **Learning transfer:** [instruction studies](research/automatic-use.md) show increased expression use in unscripted classroom speech, but lasting everyday transfer for this adult personal trainer remains unverified. Daily and Extra remain phrase recall; a distinct freer-speaking activity still needs functional design before implementation.
- **Sustained engagement:** adult missions, progress feedback and optional rewards are research-informed choices, not demonstrated long-term adherence for Phrase Gym. Keep rewards independent of memory grades and make return after a break easy.
- **First MVP:** the software loop and stopped-app copy procedure are implemented. A later real-clock return, semantic inspection of representative external cards, listening to their references and complete attended MacBook recording/replay remain unverified.
- **Practice facts:** Today counts, the studied list, the streak and next return are verified against a simulated clock. A real-clock next-day return and missed day, and the panel on a real iPhone, remain attended checks.
- **Prepared content:** representative externally prepared phrases still need inspection for intended meaning, natural/understandable cues, leakage, acceptable answers, helpful recall hints and searchable usage contexts. No fixed synthetic pack, including the documented example, establishes every course's content quality.
- **Speech:** the existing English Piper voice is a candidate, not a listening acceptance for all material. Other languages require their own voice and listening check when added. Serbian help preferences remain unresolved and do not block English.
- **Recording:** the learner has verified Safari recording/reference playback on the Mac and the iPhone recording/playback fix on the exercised path. Complete saved replay after reload and application restart, final-word completeness across varied speech lengths, headset routing, iPhone background interruptions and Daily, Extra and phrase-specific Practice, Library search, details and Delete on a real iPhone still need attended checks. Synthetic browser audio cannot certify those paths.
- **Personal data:** stopped-app copy/restore is covered with local data; the learner should retain a private backup before relying on it. This does not require concurrent recovery.
- **Later internal preparation:** echo-words sense selection and generated content need representative actual outputs and separate semantic review when that stage is implemented. No provider/platform qualification matrix is implied.

# Research evidence

Each note answers one design question. Every source has a link, what was studied, what it means for Phrase Gym and its limit. Notes summarize; they never copy papers. **Checked** states what was actually read: full text, abstract, or a secondary description. A secondary-only claim never justifies a decision on its own.

No source tests Phrase Gym's complete spoken workflow. A decision that applies these findings is a design inference and says so. Research notes describe evidence and possible implications; they are not a feature backlog. The focused decisions and delivery ladder choose the smallest application of that evidence.

## Evidence to observable behavior

| Finding and source | Observable product choice | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Kim & Webb (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12479): spacing benefits L2 learning; equal and expanding schedules were comparable overall | Cards return on later days instead of requiring repeated readback in one sitting | Does not select FSRS, 0.90 retention or a next-day first check |
| [Li & Taguchi (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1111/modl.12123): speed gains were specific to input/output practice modality | Invite the learner to say the response, with optional recording | Request forms in L2 Chinese; does not prove conversational transfer here |
| [Bardovi-Harlig, Mossman & Su (2017)](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/12991145-c5d6-4eb5-b7c4-d2a228eb8d84/content): instruction with noticing and production improved elicited oral routines | Teach wording/use first, then cue a communicative intention with an optional partner line | Combined classroom treatment; no isolated proof that situation cues outperform translation |
| [Butowska-Buczyńska et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2413511121): varied retrieval cues improved learning in the studied tasks | Rotate a small set of natural cues | Not a test of spontaneous phrase use; no optimal cue count follows |
| [Study and feedback evidence](phrase-prompts.md#study-first-when-the-phrase-is-unfamiliar) varies with prior knowledge and task | Ungraded first study, then attempted recall and prompt correction | Studying every imported card first is a simple default, not a rule that known phrases must be restudied |
| [Retrieval and self-rating evidence](spacing-and-grading.md#retrieval-and-repetition) favors continued retrieval and judging the actual attempt | Reveal, compare and rate the original response; repeated supported takes do not upgrade it | The three buttons and one graded review per day are design choices |
| [Word/context evidence](words-in-context.md) supports deliberate form/meaning learning and productive practice | Later word cards teach one sense in understandable contexts and require its spoken form | A gap task does not itself establish active conversational use |
| [Conversational-uptake studies](automatic-use.md#instruction-leading-to-independently-composed-speech) found increased expression use after instruction and speaking practice | A future freer-speaking activity needs a distinct design; repeatable bounded card recall does not supply it | Classroom conversations, small analyzed samples and combined treatments; lasting everyday transfer remains unverified |
| [Motivation evidence](motivation.md) distinguishes enjoyment, persistence and learning | Later adult missions, informative progress, forgiving weekly targets and optional rewards | No validated combination or optimum target for this personal trainer |

The first four sources have primary-source abstract or text support; their detailed notes identify reading depth. Secondary-only observations do not justify mandatory features. Exact session sizes, cue counts, scheduling defaults and interface choices remain changeable engineering decisions.

## What this does not establish

No reviewed study identifies a best personal app for making phrases spontaneous in conversation. Card recall, elicited oral production and natural opportunity/use are different outcomes. Recording/reference comparison provides a practical self-check; it is not a validated pronunciation score or proof of transfer. Forced mock dialogues, whole-script memorization, timers, dependency graphs and elaborate hint/repair machinery do not follow as necessary features.

The MVP targets spaced, varied spoken retrieval with feedback. [Extra practice and adult engagement](../decision-transfer-and-engagement.md) are planned subsequent increments, ahead of more content integrations. There is useful evidence to inform a later freer-speaking activity; lack of app-specific proof is not a reason to abandon the active-use goal. Do not make a learner effectiveness experiment a delivery requirement.

Excluded: Fang, Elgort & Chen (2024) is retracted ([publisher record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/201FABD72088A7F590598A5973E5B499)). Vendor and blog descriptions (Pimsleur, EPI) and community anecdotes are not evidence.

# Learning policy and honest evidence

Use evidence-informed defaults and ordinary self-ratings; the learner is not asked to tune parameters, run calibration or establish effectiveness.

## Scheduling choices

All lessons and selected lessons use due/new eligibility in the selected language. Prioritize oldest due targets, with up to 20 targets per session and at most five new targets filling remaining capacity. A due backlog may consume the whole budget. This limits new work without forcing early repetitions of every lesson phrase. Retired, suspended and nonpreferred alternatives are excluded from scheduled practice.

Use FSRS-6 with population parameters and desired retention 0.90; the arithmetic of the locked py-fsrs 6.3.2 release is the reference. Not yet maps to Again, With effort to Hard, and Recalled to Good. With effort means correct retrieval with difficulty, never partial recall or recognition after reveal. No Easy button is needed. Disable short steps and fuzzing because the app owns its repair queue. Let elapsed time and memory history determine intervals and lapse adjustments; there is no fixed ladder, universal one-day reset or interval ceiling.

Recorded reference sequences (first reviews, lapses, long gaps, same-day reviews and the interval cap) define scheduler equivalence. Any implementation must reproduce their states, whole-day intervals and due timestamps exactly; stability and difficulty may differ only within a relative 10⁻⁹, so floating-point noise can never move a due time or change queue eligibility. A scheduler change that breaks this equivalence requires a new policy version. Scheduler arithmetic stays separate from the eligibility policy that decides which evidence reaches it. The available Rust FSRS crates implement an older FSRS version or 32-bit arithmetic, so the Rust scheduler ports the reference arithmetic directly.

After eligible failure/help, a repair needs five minutes and two distinct other finalized targets. Cap extra attempts at two; finish a short session without a waiting timer. Repairs retain the original review group. Only its first eligible finalized result updates the schedule; later success cannot rewrite the first result or repeatedly promote it. New ungraded presentations can receive their first independent recall after that spacing or in a later session, without waiting at the end of a short session. A provisional next-day check for an unassessed target does not block this first spaced recall; early-practice exclusion still applies to assessed targets.

Rotate a phrase's current prompts across reviews: prefer the prompt with fewer presentations, then the one presented longest ago, then stable IDs. Pin prompt identity, revision and modality with each attempt.

A new confusable partner waits until its prerequisite has been recalled independently once on a later learning day, using the profile timezone. Store directed, acyclic dependencies, including links across inbox entries. Other new targets fill the place. Retiring or making a prerequisite unavailable for scheduled practice releases its introduction dependency rather than stranding the new item. A still-active prerequisite that has not been recalled leaves its dependent deferred with a visible reason; the learner can choose another preferred target or retire an unwanted prerequisite. The precise later-day gate is an engineering choice, not a measured optimum.

## Evidence that can change a schedule

The first response's modality, time and support remain immutable across comparison, help and rerecording. Answer reveal snapshots the response context before exposure; capture snapshots before recording and keeps the prompt until completion. The learner judges correctness and effort after comparison, referring to that original response. Do not freeze a correctness guess before feedback or treat a later rerecording as the original attempt. Post-response reference playback does not invalidate the earlier response.

A hint, help, reference, recent lookup or full-script exposure before responding remains support even after hiding the answer. Check support at response time as well as presentation. Preparation declares known cross-target exposures in prompts, dialogue and reference turns so a later target cannot count as independent recall after its answer was supplied elsewhere. Full dialogue runs are always rehearsal and never update FSRS; retain their ratings and recordings. Prevent an immediate independent check of rehearsed targets in the same run/session context. The incidental-lookup time window does not erase declared rehearsal exposure just because five minutes elapsed.

On an otherwise eligible independent review of a target with a memory state, needing Hint after trying records Not yet once at finalization. That is a failure of unaided recall, not a Hard success. The hint tap alone never writes a lapse. Apply technical failure, stale version, prior support, lifecycle, early-practice and consumed-review-group exclusions before this exception. A defective prompt recreated before rating or an explicit study choice does not become a forgetting observation.

Other supported ratings retain the learner's choice without becoming FSRS recall observations. A never-assessed supported target gets an honest next-day learning check without an invented success or memory state unless its first independent recall already occurred after the required spacing. Default-unfamiliar first meetings and a reveal into study on a first encounter are ungraded. Presentation completion records no proof that the learner spoke. Existing due obligations remain unresolved after other supported, technical or stale practice; suspension/retirement removes the target from the active queue without grading memory.

Interrupted capture and technical failure create no automatic forgetting grade. Deleting, changing the preferred expression or recreating before rating excludes the attempt without grading memory and invalidates stale review bindings. Compatible recreation preserves identity/history, but materially changed assessment conditions require an independent check before old recall is shown as evidence under the new conditions. Lookup recordings attach to actual exposure receipts without inventing a review session. Real-call marks and dialogue rehearsal never change a schedule.

Silent recall can supply self-reported memory evidence. Recording stays optional and remains self-assessment, with no automatic proof of pronunciation, completion, speed or fluency. No recognition-only result lengthens the productive schedule.

## Why these defaults

[Research on L2 spacing](https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12479) supports spaced returns, without selecting one universal interval ladder. [Variable-retrieval research](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2413511121) motivates rotating prompts. FSRS updates an item's estimated difficulty after each rating, but one memory state cannot tell forgetting from an unusually obscure prompt, so prompts of one phrase stay comparable in difficulty and defective ones are excluded rather than graded. [Within-session retrieval research](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263116000280) motivates bounded repetition rather than endless retries. Sources and their limits are in [spacing and grading](research/spacing-and-grading.md) and [phrase prompts](research/phrase-prompts.md).

These principles inform the policy; the exact rotation, three buttons, repair limits, introduction gate and FSRS setting are engineering choices. The [FSRS documentation](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/py-fsrs) describes the scheduler, not evidence that it is optimal for this whole-phrase workflow. Predicted retention under a declared prompt is not conversational fluency or a guaranteed success rate. Keeping rehearsed dialogues separate avoids assuming they are equivalent to independent recall.

# Core trainer policy and evidence — P3

2026-10-12. The user requested implementation of the whole P3 core and delegated routine learning-policy choices to the implementer using the existing research. The [unified-card decision](decision-unified-practice-card.md) remains the interaction authority. This implements the practical defaults from the [research/review design](design/answer-card-and-review-policy.md); it is not a claim of learner-effectiveness validation.

## Sessions and learning policy

- All lessons and selected lessons use due/new targets in the URL language, with one schedule per target. Sessions contain up to 20 targets, prioritizing oldest due work and filling remaining capacity with at most five new targets. A due backlog can consume the entire budget. Lesson lists remain complete and reference lookup creates no grade.
- Manifests pin content/lessons, cue IDs, hidden required chunks, target schedule versions, review groups, reference/cue/partner media IDs, schema/planner/policy versions and prepared dialogue bindings. Sessions never change language through navigation. The practice URL selects the displayed/addressed session independently of the open session in the launcher; historical superseded sessions show retained results. Queued UI actions bind their initiating session/step and cannot become an action on a later card. Conflicting journal events are retained separately without resequencing and the client reloads authoritative state before another choice. Resume is explicit; another session requires explicit takeover. Superseded history and late reports remain stored without schedule effects.
- The cue selector excludes reports against the pinned cue revision. Among cues hiding the same number of required chunks, it prioritizes low recall/high failure coverage, fewer presentations, older presentations and stable cue IDs. Word/frame cues must hide their required target material. Presentation and eligible recall/failure coverage persist across sessions; no complete-coverage interval gate is imposed.
- The adapter pins `fsrs==6.3.2`, population parameters, desired retention 0.90 and Again/Hard/Good for Not yet/With effort/Recalled. Library short learning/relearning steps and interval fuzzing are disabled. Actual elapsed time, adaptive intervals and lapse adjustments come from the [maintained library](https://open-spaced-repetition.github.io/py-fsrs/); receipts retain policy/parameters and before/after memory state. There is no fixed ladder, universal one-day lapse reset or 30-day ceiling.
- Supported ratings retain the learner's choice but supply no FSRS recall observation. A never-assessed supported target receives a next-day learning check without invented success or memory state. An existing due obligation remains unresolved after supported/technical/unclear/stale practice. A policy mismatch cannot silently update an old session.
- Eligible failure/help can enqueue a repair after five minutes and two distinct other finalized targets, capped at two extra attempts. If the session ends first, it completes without a waiting timer. Technical failures, stale material and reported cues do not manufacture memory repairs. Repairs retain the original review group and result; only the first eligible finalized result updates FSRS.

## One card and honest evidence

The card renders authored chunk masks and prompts; never random gaps. Optional capture or Show answer reveals the whole phrase in place. The distinct blue book action expands native-language meaning/use/chunk material inline. Three colored/icon ratings save and advance quietly. A collapsible criteria explanation distinguishes correct effortful recall from partial recall and permits declared equivalents. No recording switch, problem submenu, Practice again, supported-only Continue, saved-result banner or Undo returns.

The first response is immutable. Show answer snapshots self-reported recall before exposing the model; recording snapshots its original modality/support before capture, keeps the gaps during permission/capture and reveals only on capture completion. An unfinished capture remains technically ineligible after reload, even before recovered bytes reach the server. Explanation/reference help before that snapshot, recent lookup exposure and recent full-script exposure remain support. Support is checked again at response time, including lookup between presentation and response. Recent on-card help is dated from its acknowledged reveal/explanation/reference/capture-completion event receipt, not presentation or finalization of the card. Later takes or comparison cannot relabel the original. Recorded modality is capture/self-report evidence, not automatic speech recognition or proof of fluency. Technical failures create no automatic memory grade.

The server derives target/content/cue/group/base-schedule bindings from the addressed manifest step. Ordered events and receipts commit atomically with any allowed schedule change, using event IDs, payload conflict checks, monotonic sequence and serialized schedule-version checks. Client occurrence time is bounded by issued-session/receipt time with a five-minute skew allowance and monotonic adjustment; corrections are labeled in receipts. Clients never submit due dates or authoritative grades.

The administrative cue-report event can exclude an unclear revision without a forgetting grade. It has no learner problem/skip submenu; the final P1 simplification is preserved. Changed published revisions get independent cue-report eligibility while retaining target identity.

## Audio and durable recovery

Every take is a separate SQLite BLOB with immutable ID, hash, actual MIME, timestamps, duration when available, completeness and non-identifying capture settings. Complete containers are decoded locally with cross-platform PyAV and checked against declared MIME. Recoverable interrupted bytes are retained as incomplete even when the container cannot finalize. Reference audio remains the existing original-byte media service; no new synthesis/provider call or external upload is involved.

The microphone requests permission only after a recording action, becomes Stop during capture/playback, and has a stop instruction only during capture. Capture disables ratings while permission/capture/processing is active. A microphone stream is reused between takes and released on pause, navigation, Settings, background and unmount; late permission streams are released. Final recorder data is flushed before release. Capture failure leaves self-check available and cannot become an automatic forgetting observation. Playback uses one adapter, with learner-then-reference comparison and explicit replay/failure recovery.

IndexedDB journals immutable events, acknowledged session checkpoints and pending takes using ArrayBuffers. Capture snapshots arriving approximately once per second are retained; abrupt page/process termination can recover the bytes already journaled, not promise the unflushed tail. Live capture owners are checked across tabs before marking an abandoned take incomplete. Foreground resume and explicit Retry resend the same IDs. Take entries are removed only after matching database ID/hash/byte-count/completeness acknowledgement. A rejected take retains its bytes and its own visible error while independent valid transfers continue. Capture stops with an interruption warning at 8 MiB to leave final-encoder headroom below the 12 MiB server limit; this resource boundary does not implement silence-based stopping. Storage failure remains visible; unacknowledged data is never expired or replaced by a later take. A cached checkpoint can display retained evidence during a backend outage; this is not a downloaded offline pack or service-worker release.

Lookup recordings attach to actual reference exposures, with no invented review session. Drill takes also bind their capture event; its receipt preserves support for that individual take separately from the original response. Latest-take replay orders by capture time, including pending local bytes. Future backups include the BLOBs; backup/restore acceptance belongs to P6.

## Prepared dialogues and limits

Dedicated rehearsal pins the complete prepared exchange and target bindings. Displaying the introduction records full-script exposure before any response; beginning rehearsal records the recent exposure again. Partner/supporting lines and supplied partner audio remain available. Isolating a target turn hides surrounding lines while preserving its place; the next target restores the dialogue cursor. Repeated target turns share their review group/schedule. The complete exchange remains visible in results. No open-ended chat or role-play endpoint is added.

This is the local core trainer with reviewed manual packages. Actual Safari/headset clipping (AUDIO-01), iPhone/PWA acceptance, private HTTPS and actual actor/Serbian listening/content acceptance remain in [open problems](open-problems.md). P4 owns downloaded-session/offline and device hardening, P5 owns intake/LLM/speech preparation and external sources, and P6 owns deployment/backups. No closed P1/P2 review loop is reopened.

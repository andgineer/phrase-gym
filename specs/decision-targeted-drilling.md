# Product scope and cues

Phrase Gym trains selected words, fixed expressions and prepared dialogues toward ready use. It is a personal trainer, with no open-ended conversation, role-play or learner effectiveness experiments.

## What is being retrieved

The response is the whole phrase. Fixed expressions have declared acceptable equivalents; word/frame targets identify the required sense or construction and the slots that may vary. Different meanings are different targets. Examples, cue variants, lesson memberships and dialogue appearances share one target and schedule.

A situation alone cannot identify one exact sentence. Each retrieval cue must retain meaningful phrase-specific wording that distinguishes the learned expression from plausible alternatives. Required disambiguation is visible from the start. There is no first-words rule, random masking or letter puzzle. Word/frame cues hide the required target material; a visible target is supported use, not retrieval of that target.

Prepare and check cues before submission. Different hidden chunks are useful only when each cue stays clear. One reliable cue is enough; without one, retain supported lookup/practice and leave retrieval unready. The app's structural checks cannot certify linguistic quality.

New database material may already be familiar from its source, so no mandatory introduction precedes every drill. Help remains available before responding. Prepared dialogues retain complete exchanges and explicitly identified target turns; isolating a difficult turn preserves its place and schedule.

## Interaction constraints

One Start session action uses all lessons or a selected lesson. Starting explicitly replaces the known open session while retaining its history; Resume preserves the existing position. Browsing or changing language never replaces a session. Phrase lookup searches text and prepared concepts across the language's lessons, opens reference material and links to complete lesson lists. Browsing alone never grades recall.

Keep one compact practice card with optional recording, answer reveal, inline native help, replay and three self-check choices. Ratings save and advance quietly. The microphone directly starts another take. No recording-mode switch, problem submenu, Practice again, supported-only Continue, saved-rating banner or Undo UI. Show & explain appears only when useful help exists in the selected native language; translation alone is sufficient and missing help has no placeholder.

Native language supplies explanations. Learning language belongs to bookmarkable URLs and scopes content/session views. UI follows either native or current learning language, with no independent UI-language selector. Explicit URLs win over best-effort device restoration; navigation makes no Settings writes. Settings saves one complete native/UI preference at a time per tab, retaining failed drafts. Live cross-tab Settings synchronization is outside scope.

Automatic stopping, speech grading, historical recording-comparison UI and locked-screen/eyes-free operation are deferred.

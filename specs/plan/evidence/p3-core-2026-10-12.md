## Limits

No test uses the operator's microphone, private recordings, `.env`, real provider, source mutation or external delivery. Synthetic reference/capture audio proves transport/state/decoding behavior, not speech quality or actual Safari/headset/iPhone behavior. AUDIO-01 and real actor/Serbian content acceptance remain open; no actual-device clipping investigation or P1/P2 review loop is restarted.

The core runs with prepared manual packages. Service workers/downloaded offline packs (P4), real intake/LLM/speech preparation (P5), and deployment/backups/restore (P6) are outside this milestone. No push or deployment is authorized by the local commit. The initial sample content has structural review flags and synthetic test assets; it is not a certified release language pack.

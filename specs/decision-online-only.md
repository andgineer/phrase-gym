# Online-only practice

Practice, lookup, library and Settings require the live server. Server reads and receipts own card position, support, repairs, lifecycle and results. No downloads, offline installation, cached trainer or predicted local progression.

Connection failure hides normal learning/Settings and blocks new actions. Already active media remains stoppable/cancellable, preserving interrupted bytes under their initiating context. Retry resends original pending writes and reloads authoritative state before enabling learning. Browser connectivity signals are hints, not proof of recovery. Keep this a small error/retry flow, without per-action health probes or a general availability framework.

IndexedDB retains initiated writes, acknowledged checkpoints and pending takes for retry. Preserve original IDs, payloads, order, support and capture bindings. Conflicts remain retained without resequencing; a new action uses the reconciled server card. These journals protect evidence, not offline practice or guaranteed survival of browser eviction.

Client upgrades remove only owned obsolete caches/registrations and pack/display data. The retirement worker at the old URL is network-only and unregisters itself. Neither cleanup path clears pending evidence, unrelated origin storage or reloads active/volatile audio. Narrow decoding of old queued payloads preserves their receipt identity; new contracts expose no offline behavior.
